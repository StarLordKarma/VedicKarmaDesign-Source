Postiz Local

A private, self-hosted publishing workspace for the owner's YouTube channels and, when connected, Instagram accounts. It prepares media, manages a publishing calendar and retrieves performance metrics.

Access is limited to the owner and personally known account holders. The publishing server runs on the owner's Mac. This public page provides information only; it does not expose the administration interface.

YouTube access is used to identify connected channels, upload and manage videos, apply video metadata and thumbnails, and retrieve authorized analytics. Instagram is not currently connected.
