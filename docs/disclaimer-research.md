# Disclaimer research notes

The implementation uses cautious, plain-language wording rather than claiming complete immunity from liability.

1. Comparable astrology/spiritual services commonly state that readings are for reflection/spiritual guidance, are not medical/legal/financial advice, and do not guarantee predictions or outcomes. Example: Embodied Astrology's public disclaimer separates astrology from medical/psychological treatment and directs users to qualified professionals: https://www.embodiedastrology.com/disclaimer-waiver
2. EU consumer guidance states that standard consumer terms must be fair, plain, and understandable; terms that create an imbalance or improperly exclude a trader's liability may be non-binding. This is why the site disclaimer preserves mandatory consumer rights and legally non-excludable liability instead of saying the operator is never responsible: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/unfair-treatment/unfair-contract-terms/index_en.htm

These sources informed the EN/RU/DE/ES copy in shared/i18n.ts and the customer email wording in server/client-delivery.ts. The text is a product disclaimer draft, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice; attorney review is recommended before relying on it.
