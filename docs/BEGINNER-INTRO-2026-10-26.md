# Beginner introductions and warm zodiac palette

The three landing sections now explain their origins and approach in English, Russian, Spanish and German. Vedic and Western each have two short paragraphs; Tarot has one. The copy distinguishes this site's stellar-reference Vedic zodiac from its seasonal tropical Western zodiac without describing astrology as a scientifically established prediction method.

Zodiac buttons retain their square shape, symbols, translated names, dates, selected/focus states and responsive grid. The former pale blue is replaced by ivory, parchment and bronze: backgrounds `#fff8e8`, `#f2e6d0`, `#eadbc0`; symbols/focus `#745226`; labels `#493720`; dates `#604b32`. This is a warm analogous treatment chosen to match the existing gold and beige atlas, rather than a claim that a colour wheel prescribes one uniquely correct colour.
